Openings

We are looking for motivated undergraduate and graduate students in physics, chemistry, and engineering to join our research team. Our projects span a diverse range of topics, including ultrafast molecular spectroscopy, materials characterization, and nonlinear optics. We design and build our own research equipment, giving students the opportunity to work hands-on with cutting-edge experimental techniques and instrumentation. Through this experience, you will develop valuable technical and professional skills that are highly sought after in the rapidly growing fields of photonics and quantum technology.

BSRA-SRNL Graduate Scholar Program (2-year GRA position)

We have an immediate opening for a graduate research assistantship position in collaboration with Savannah River National Lab (SRNL). The project involves spectroscopic characterization of different materials that support fusion energy and hydrogen isotope science, and additive manufacturing. The materials will be fabricated at SRNL and are centered around novel chemistry for two-photon polymerization (2PP), specifically photoinitiated ring-opening metathesis polymerization (ROMP), to be used for 3d printing and fabrication. Ultrafast spectroscopy, such as transient UV-vis absorption spectroscopy and femtosecond stimulated Raman spectroscopy, which are available in our lab at UGA, will provide mechanistic insights into the fundamental photochemical processes that initiate ring-opening and polymerization. For more information please email ullrich@uga.edu
